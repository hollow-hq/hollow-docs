Hollow's music engine delivers high-performance, crystal-clear audio streaming directly into your Discord voice channels. Powered by high-speed audio nodes, Hollow supports search queries, playlists, volume normalization, and real-time DSP filter presets.

---

## Getting Started with Music

To start listening, join any voice channel in your Discord server and execute:

```text
,play lofi hip hop radio
```
or provide a direct URL:
```text
,play https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfKfPfyJRdk
```

Hollow will automatically connect to your voice channel, enqueue the track or playlist, and begin playback.

---

## Playback Commands

| Command | Syntax | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `play` | `,play <search or URL>` | Searches for tracks or enqueues playlists. |
| `skip` | `,skip` | Skips the currently playing track. |
| `stop` | `,stop` | Stops playback, flushes the queue, and leaves the voice channel. |
| `volume` | `,volume <1-100>` | Adjusts output volume percentage. |
| `preset` | `,preset [name]` | Applies real-time audio equalization/filter presets. |

## Audio Filter Presets

Transform your listening experience with real-time DSP audio filters:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="8D Audio" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Spatial rotation audio filter that pans sound dynamically across left and right stereo channels.
    ```text
    ,preset 8d
    ```
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nightcore" icon="bolt-lightning">
    Increases audio speed and pitch by 1.3x for high-energy playback.
    ```text
    ,preset nightcore
    ```
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daycore (Slowed)" icon="moon">
    Slows down audio rate and lowers pitch by 0.7x for a relaxed, chopped atmosphere.
    ```text
    ,preset daycore
    ```
  </Card>
  <Card title="Earrape / Distortion" icon="volume-high">
    Overdrives audio gain and applies harmonic sinusoidal distortion.
    ```text
    ,preset earrape
    ```
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To clear all active audio filters and restore original studio playback:
```text
,preset off
```

## Inactivity & Timeout Behavior

To preserve bandwidth and resources:
- If Hollow's music queue becomes empty and no new tracks are enqueued within **10 seconds**, the player automatically disconnects from the voice channel and resets the bot's status presence.
- If all human members leave the voice channel, the music player is marked inactive and will disconnect.
