## 2. Expression Tools (Emoji Management)

Quickly expand your server's emoji collection without downloading and re-uploading files:

- `,emoji steal <emoji or URL> [name]` — Clones a custom emoji into your server. Requires `Manage Expressions`.
- `,emoji remove <emoji>` — Deletes a custom emoji from your server.
- `,emoji <emoji>` — Enlarges an emoji and displays its internal ID and CDN URL.

---

## 3. Message Sniping

Recover messages that were recently deleted or edited, as well as removed reactions:

- `,snipe [index]` (Alias: `,s`) — Views the most recently deleted messages in the channel (supports index 1-10).
- `,editsnipe [index]` (Alias: `,es`) — Views previous versions of edited messages.
- `,reactionsnipe` — Shows the most recently removed emoji reactions.

---

## 4. General Utilities

- `,afk [reason]` — Sets your AFK status. When anyone mentions you, Hollow informs them you're away and how long you've been gone. When you speak again, Hollow welcomes you back.
- `,urban <word>` — Looks up definitions on Urban Dictionary.
- `,quickpoll` (Alias: `,qp`) — Quickly appends thumbs-up reactions to your message for informal polls.
- `,pin <message link>` / `,unpin <message link>` — Pin and unpin messages without needing native desktop context menus.
- `,suggest <text>` — Sends a community feature suggestion.
- `,donate` — Displays contribution links to help cover Hollow's hosting costs.
