Hollow features deep integration with the **Last.fm** music scrobbling network. Connect your Last.fm username to share what you're listening to, inspect your scrobble statistics, and discover new artists based on your listening habits.

---

## Linking Your Last.fm Account

To link your Discord account to your Last.fm profile:

```text
,lastfm set YourUsername
```

Hollow immediately verifies that your username exists on Last.fm. If verified, your profile is stored and linked to your Discord ID.

To unlink or clear your account:
```text
,lastfm reset
```

## Available Commands

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Now Playing" icon="play">
    Display the track you or another member are currently listening to, including album art, artist scrobbles, and total scrobble count.
    ```text
    ,lastfm nowplaying
    ,lastfm np @member
    ```
  </Card>

  <Card title="Scrobbles Overview" icon="chart-simple">
    View lifetime scrobbles, unique artists played, tracks played, and account registration age.
    ```text
    ,lastfm scrobbles
    ,lastfm scrobbles @member
    ```
  </Card>

  <Card title="Top Artists Leaderboard" icon="ranking-star">
    Interactive paginated list of your top 50 all-time most played artists with exact play counts.
    ```text
    ,lastfm topartists
    ,lastfm topartists @member
    ```
  </Card>

  <Card title="Music Recommendations" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Generates personalized artist recommendations by sampling your top artists and discovering similar musicians.
    ```text
    ,lastfm recommendation
    ```
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Profile Lookup (`whois` / `info`)

Inspect detailed Last.fm profile information for yourself or any other server member who has linked their account:

```text
,lastfm whois @voby
,lastfm info
```

Displays:
- Linked Last.fm username and avatar
- Lifetime scrobbles
- Country of origin
- Account creation timestamp
