Public Discord communities, gaming guilds, and large creators are high-value targets for coordinated raiding groups. This guide explains how to tune Hollow's anti-raid modules to strike the ideal balance between security and newcomer friction.

---

## Recommended Anti-Raid Tiers

Depending on the size and exposure of your server, choose a security profile:

| Profile | Mass Join Threshold | Account Age Filter | Default Avatar | Recommended For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard** | 8 joins / 10s (`kick`) | 1 day (`kick`) | Disabled | Private / Small Community |
| **Hardened** | 5 joins / 10s (`ban`) | 3 days (`kick`) | Enabled (`kick`) | Growing / Public Guild |
| **Lockdown** | 3 joins / 10s (`ban`) | 7 days (`ban`) | Enabled (`ban`) | Under Active Attack |

## Hardened Configuration Walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure Mass Join Defense">
    Set mass join detection to ban attackers when 5 or more accounts flood the server within 10 seconds:
    ```text
    ,antiraid massjoin enable --do ban --threshold 5
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable New Account Quarantine">
    Filter accounts younger than 3 days:
    ```text
    ,antiraid newaccounts on --do kick --threshold 3
    ```
    *If a legitimate friend joins on an alt account, staff can easily whitelist them:*
    ```text
    ,antiraid newaccounts whitelist @Friend
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Default Avatar Filter">
    Reject accounts that have not uploaded a profile picture:
    ```text
    ,antiraid defaultavatar enable --do kick
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Lockdown Role and Channel Exclusions">
    In the event you need to freeze all chat channels simultaneously:
    1. Set the role to be locked:
       ```text
       ,lockdown role @Member
       ```
    2. Add staff channels and announcement channels to the exclusion list:
       ```text
       ,lockdown ignore #staff-chat
       ,lockdown ignore #announcements
       ```
    3. Verify your ignored list:
       ```text
       ,lockdown ignored
       ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Do During an Active Raid

If your community is actively being spammed:

1. **Trigger Emergency Lockdown:**
   ```text
   ,lockdown all
   ```
   *This immediately strips send message permissions across every channel in your server except ignored staff channels.*

2. **Bulk-Purge Raider Messages:**
   ```text
   ,purge links 100
   ,purge bots 100
   ,purge mentions 50
   ```

3. **Check Raid Stats:**
   ```text
   ,antiraid settings
   ```

4. **Unlock Once Safe:**
   ```text
   ,unlock all
   ```
