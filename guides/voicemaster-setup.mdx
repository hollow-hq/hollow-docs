Voice channels that sit empty clutter your server sidebar. VoiceMaster replaces static channels with a single, elegant "Join to Create" generator that spawns private rooms on demand.

---

## Deployment Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Execute Setup">
    Run the setup command in any channel:
    ```text
    ,voicemaster setup
    ```
    Hollow will create:
    - A voice category named `voice`
    - A text channel named `interface` containing the interactive controller embed and buttons
    - A voice channel named `Join to Create`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Channel Permissions">
    Make sure Hollow has the following permissions in the created category:
    - `Manage Channels`
    - `Move Members`
    - `Connect`
    - `View Channel`
    
    Ensure `@everyone` has permission to **View Channel** and **Connect** on the `Join to Create` channel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test the System">
    1. Join the **Join to Create** voice channel in Discord.
    2. Hollow will instantly create a new channel named `<YourUsername>'s Channel` and move you into it.
    3. Open the `#interface` channel. You can click the 🔒 **Lock**, 👁️ **Hide**, or 👥 **Limit** buttons to manage your channel.
    4. Disconnect from the voice channel. Within seconds of the room becoming empty, Hollow will delete it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Best Practices

- **Channel Position:** You can freely rename or drag the `voice` category anywhere in your Discord channel list. Hollow tracks category and channel IDs in its database.
- **Interface Access:** Keep `#interface` viewable by all server members, but set `Send Messages` to **False** for `@everyone`. Members only need permission to interact with buttons, not chat in the interface channel.
- **Custom Bitrate:** If your server has Nitro Boost Level 1, 2, or 3, members can increase their voice bitrate up to 128kbps, 256kbps, or 384kbps using `,voicemaster bitrate`.
