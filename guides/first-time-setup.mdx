Setting up a new Discord server can be overwhelming. Follow this step-by-step checklist to configure moderation roles, automated join greetings, verification roles, and raid defenses in under 10 minutes.

---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Establish Role Hierarchy">
    Before running any commands, open Discord's **Server Settings -> Roles**:
    1. Drag the **Hollow** integration role above your `@Moderator` and member roles.
    2. Ensure Hollow possesses the **Administrator** permission (or at least `Manage Roles`, `Manage Channels`, and `Moderate Members`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Your Guild Prefix & Aliases">
    Pick a prefix that works for your staff team:
    ```text
    ,prefix add !
    ```
    Set up convenient shortcuts for moderation:
    ```text
    !alias add p purge
    !alias add m mute
    !alias add um unmute
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Staff Permissions with Fakeperms">
    Instead of giving native Discord administrative rights to your moderation staff, assign them fake permissions inside Hollow:
    ```text
    !fakeperms add @Moderator moderate_members
    !fakeperms add @Moderator manage_messages
    !fakeperms add @Moderator kick_members
    !fakeperms add @Moderator ban_members
    ```
    Verify the configuration:
    ```text
    !fakeperms list @Moderator
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Member Onboarding (Autorole & Welcome)">
    Automatically assign your community role when new members arrive:
    ```text
    !autorole add @Member
    ```
    Create a clean welcome message in your `#welcome` channel:
    ```text
    !welcome add #welcome {embed}$v{title: Welcome to {guild.name}!}$v{description: Hey {user.mention}, thank you for joining us! Please check our rules in <#123456789012345678> before chatting.}$v{thumbnail: {user.avatar}}$v{color: #7d7ead}$v{footer: Member #{guild.count.format}}
    ```
    Test how it renders:
    ```text
    !welcome view #welcome
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Basic Anti-Raid Protection">
    Protect your server from immediate join raids and alt account botnets:
    ```text
    # Stop mass joins (kick if more than 5 users join in 10s)
    !antiraid massjoin enable --do kick --threshold 5

    # Filter brand-new accounts younger than 3 days
    !antiraid newaccounts on --do kick --threshold 3
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Sticky Channel Rules">
    Keep your rules or verification guidelines visible at the bottom of your busy chat channels:
    ```text
    !stickymessage add #general Remember to follow server rules and keep discussion respectful!
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're Ready!

Your server is now protected by Hollow's automated security defenses, equipped with an onboarding flow, and configured with safe moderation permissions.
