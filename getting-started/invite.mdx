Adding Hollow to your server takes less than a minute. This guide walks through the required authorization scopes, recommended permissions, and post-invite checklist.

## Quick Invite Links

<Tip>
  We recommend using the **Administrator** invite link for convenience so Hollow never runs into missing permission errors when creating channels, managing roles, or executing purge filters.
</Tip>

- **Full Permission Invite (Recommended):**  
  [Add Hollow with Administrator](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1447869784937988201&permissions=8&scope=bot%20applications.commands)
- **Standard Invite (Minimal permissions granted per role):**  
  [Add Hollow (Custom Permissions)](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1447869784937988201&permissions=1099511627775&scope=bot%20applications.commands)

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Authorization Link">
    Click either of the invite links above. If you are not already logged in to Discord in your browser, log in with an account that has **Manage Server** or **Administrator** rights on your target guild.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Your Guild">
    In the dropdown menu labeled **Add to Server**, choose the community where you want to install Hollow, then click **Continue**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Permissions & Authorize">
    Review the requested permission flags. Hollow requests permissions necessary for moderation, anti-raid monitoring, dynamic voice channels, and utility lookups. Click **Authorize** and complete the quick Discord captcha verification.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Adjust Role Hierarchy in Server Settings">
    In your Discord server:
    1. Go to **Server Settings** -> **Roles**.
    2. Drag the **Hollow** role up so that it sits **above** normal member, moderator, and vanity roles.
    3. Save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Discord enforces a strict rule: **Bots cannot modify, assign, kick, or ban members whose highest role is equal to or higher than the bot's highest role.** If Hollow's role is at the bottom of your role list, commands like `,ban`, `,kick`, and `,autorole` will fail.
</Warning>

## Required OAuth2 Scopes

When inviting Hollow, the following OAuth2 scopes are requested:

| Scope | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `bot` | Allows Hollow to join your guild, read events, and send messages. |
| `applications.commands` | Registers Hollow's slash commands in your server's chat box. |
