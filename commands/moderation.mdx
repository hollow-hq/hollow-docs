## Member Sanctions

### `ban`
Bans a user from the guild with an interactive confirmation menu.

- **Usage:** `,ban <user> [reason]`
- **User Permissions:** `Ban Members` (or `fakeperms` `ban_members`)
- **Bot Permissions:** `Ban Members`
- **Example:** `,ban @spammer Raiding general chat`

<ParamField path="user" type="Member / User" required>
  The member or Discord ID to ban.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="reason" type="string" default="No reason provided.">
  Reason for the ban (logged in audit logs).
</ParamField>

---

### `unban`
Unbans a previously banned user by their ID.

- **Usage:** `,unban <user_id> [reason]`
- **User Permissions:** `Moderate Members` / `Ban Members`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Ban Members`
- **Example:** `,unban 1470775670262202590 Apology accepted`

---

### `kick`
Kicks a member from the guild with an interactive confirmation prompt.

- **Usage:** `,kick <member> [reason]`
- **User Permissions:** `Kick Members`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Kick Members`
- **Example:** `,kick @voby spamming`

---

### `mute`
Places a member in a Discord timeout for a specified duration.

- **Usage:** `,mute <member> [duration] [reason]`
- **User Permissions:** `Moderate Members`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Moderate Members`
- **Example:** `,mute @member 10m Inappropriate language`

---

### `unmute`
Removes an active timeout from a member.

- **Usage:** `,unmute <member>`
- **User Permissions:** `Moderate Members`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Moderate Members`
- **Example:** `,unmute @member`

---

### `imute` / `iunmute`
Revokes or restores a member's permission to attach files and embed links.

- **Usage:** `,imute <member>` / `,iunmute <member>`
- **User Permissions:** `Moderate Members`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Manage Channels`

---

### `reactionmute` / `reactionunmute`
Revokes or restores a member's permission to add emoji reactions.

- **Usage:** `,reactionmute <member>` / `,reactionunmute <member>`
- **User Permissions:** `Moderate Members`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Manage Channels`

---

### `forcenickname`
Enforces a nickname on a member. If the member attempts to change it, Hollow automatically reverts it back.

- **Usage:** `,forcenickname <member> [nickname]`
- **User Permissions:** `Manage Nicknames`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Manage Nicknames`
- **Example:** `,forcenickname @member ModeratedName` (Run without a nickname to remove enforcement).

---

### `stripstaff`
Emergency staff revocation command that strips all administrative and moderation roles from a target member.

- **Usage:** `,stripstaff <member>`
- **User Permissions:** `Administrator`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Manage Roles`

## Channel Management

### `lockdown` / `unlock`
Locks or unlocks text channels to prevent messages from `@everyone` (or a designated lock role).

- `,lockdown [channel] [reason]` — Locks a specific channel (defaults to current).
- `,lockdown all` — Locks every channel in the server except ignored channels.
- `,lockdown ignore <channel>` — Adds or removes a channel from the lockdown exclusion list.
- `,lockdown ignored` — Lists all excluded channels.
- `,lockdown role <role>` — Sets the target role modified during lockdowns (default: `@everyone`).
- `,unlock [channel] [reason]` — Unlocks a channel.
- `,unlock all` — Unlocks all channels in the server.

### `nuke`
Duplicates the current channel, copies permissions and positions, and deletes the original channel to completely purge chat history.

- **Usage:** `,nuke [channel]`
- **User Permissions:** `Manage Guild`
- **Bot Permissions:** `Manage Channels`

### `hide` / `reveal`
Hides or reveals a channel by updating read permissions for `@everyone`.

- **Usage:** `,hide [channel]` / `,reveal [channel]`
- **User Permissions:** `Manage Channels`

## Purge Commands

Bulk-delete messages matching specific criteria (default amount: 15 messages):

- `,purge [amount]` — Deletes recent messages.
- `,purge user <@member> [amount]` — Deletes messages sent by a specific user.
- `,purge bots [amount]` — Deletes messages sent by bots.
- `,purge links [amount]` — Deletes messages containing URLs.
- `,purge attachments [amount]` — Deletes messages containing images or files.
- `,purge humans [amount]` — Deletes messages from non-bot users.
- `,purge stickers [amount]` — Deletes messages containing Discord stickers.
- `,purge mentions [amount]` — Deletes messages containing user or role mentions.

## Role Commands

- `,role <member> <role>` — Toggles a role on a user.
- `,role create <name>` — Creates a new server role.
- `,role delete <role>` — Deletes an existing role.
- `,role all <role>` — Mass-assigns a role to every member in the server.
- `,role humans <role>` — Mass-assigns a role to human members only.
- `,role color <hex> <role>` — Updates a role's color (e.g. `,role color #FF0000 @Staff`).
- `,role hoist <role>` — Toggles whether a role is hoisted separately in the member list.
- `,role mentionable <role>` — Toggles whether a role can be mentioned by everyone.
