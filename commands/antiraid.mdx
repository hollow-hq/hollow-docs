## Status & Configuration

### `antiraid settings`
Displays an overview embed showing whether each protection layer is active, its punishment type (`kick` or `ban`), and its trigger threshold.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid settings` (Aliases: `,antiraid stats`, `,antiraid config`)
- **Example output:** Shows status for Mass Join, Default Avatar, and New Accounts.

---

## Mass Join Protection

### `antiraid massjoin enable`
Enables automatic punishment when an influx of members joins within 10 seconds.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid massjoin enable --do <kick|ban> --threshold <number>`
- **Aliases:** `,antiraid massjoin on`
- **Example:** `,antiraid massjoin enable --do ban --threshold 5`

<ParamField path="--do" type="string" required>
  Action to take on raiders: `kick` or `ban`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="--threshold" type="integer" required>
  Number of joins within 10 seconds that triggers mass join protection.
</ParamField>

### `antiraid massjoin disable`
Disables mass join detection.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid massjoin disable` (Alias: `,antiraid massjoin off`)

## New Accounts Protection

### `antiraid newaccounts on`
Enables automatic punishment for accounts younger than a specified number of days.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid newaccounts on --do <kick|ban> --threshold <days>`
- **Aliases:** `,antiraid newaccounts enable`
- **Example:** `,antiraid newaccounts on --do kick --threshold 7`

<ParamField path="--do" type="string" required>
  Action to take: `kick` or `ban`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="--threshold" type="integer" required>
  Account age threshold in days.
</ParamField>

### `antiraid newaccounts disable`
Disables young account filtering.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid newaccounts disable` (Alias: `,antiraid newaccounts off`)

### `antiraid newaccounts whitelist`
Allows a specific young account to join the server without being kicked or banned.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid newaccounts whitelist <user>`
- **Example:** `,antiraid newaccounts whitelist @Friend`

## Default Avatar Protection

### `antiraid defaultavatar enable`
Filters accounts that join without a custom profile avatar.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid defaultavatar enable --do <kick|ban>`
- **Aliases:** `,antiraid defaultavatar on`
- **Example:** `,antiraid defaultavatar enable --do kick`

<ParamField path="--do" type="string" required>
  Action to take: `kick` or `ban`.
</ParamField>

### `antiraid defaultavatar disable`
Disables default avatar filtering.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid defaultavatar disable` (Alias: `,antiraid defaultavatar off`)

---

## Whitelisting

### `antiraid whitelist`
Exempts a member from all anti-raid automated actions.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid whitelist <member>` (Alias: `,antiraid wl`)
- **Example:** `,antiraid whitelist @voby`

### `antiraid unwhitelist`
Removes a member from the anti-raid exemption list.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid unwhitelist <member>` (Alias: `,antiraid unwl`)

### `antiraid whitelist view`
Displays a list of all currently whitelisted users.

- **Usage:** `,antiraid whitelist view`
