## Account Management

### `lastfm set`
Links your Discord account to your Last.fm username.

- **Usage:** `,lastfm set <username>`
- **Example:** `,lastfm set voby`
- **Cooldown:** 3 seconds

<ParamField path="username" type="string" required>
  Your registered Last.fm username.
</ParamField>

### `lastfm reset`
Unlinks your Last.fm profile from Hollow.

- **Usage:** `,lastfm reset`

## Listening & Charts

### `lastfm nowplaying`
Displays the track you or another member are currently playing (or most recently played), including album artwork and total scrobble counts.

- **Usage:** `,lastfm nowplaying [@member]`
- **Aliases:** `,lastfm np`, `,lastfm playing`, `,lastfm now`, `,fm`
- **Example:** `,lastfm np`

### `lastfm scrobbles`
Displays lifetime scrobble statistics, unique artist count, track count, and album count.

- **Usage:** `,lastfm scrobbles [@member]`

### `lastfm topartists`
Generates a paginated leaderboard of your top 50 all-time most played artists with play counts.

- **Usage:** `,lastfm topartists [@member]`

### `lastfm recommendation`
Generates personalized artist recommendations based on random samples from your top artists library.

- **Usage:** `,lastfm recommendation` (Alias: `,lastfm recommend`)

### `lastfm whois`
Displays a comprehensive profile card for a member's Last.fm account, including country and registration date.

- **Usage:** `,lastfm whois [@member]` (Alias: `,lastfm info`)
