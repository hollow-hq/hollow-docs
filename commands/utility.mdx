## Chat & Message Utilities

### `afk`
Sets an AFK status message. Whenever another member mentions you, Hollow replies with your reason and elapsed time. When you speak again, your AFK status is cleared.

- **Usage:** `,afk [reason]`
- **Example:** `,afk stepping out for dinner`

### `snipe`
Shows the most recently deleted message in the channel.

- **Usage:** `,snipe [index]` (Alias: `,s`)
- **Example:** `,snipe 2` *(views second most recently deleted message)*

### `editsnipe`
Shows the previous unedited content of a message that was modified.

- **Usage:** `,editsnipe [index]` (Alias: `,es`)

### `reactionsnipe`
Displays the most recently removed emoji reactions and who removed them.

- **Usage:** `,reactionsnipe`

### `pin` / `unpin`
Pins or unpins a message without requiring desktop context menus.

- **Usage:** `,pin <message_link>` / `,unpin <message_link>`
- **Permissions:** `Manage Messages`

## Expression & Emoji Tools

### `emoji steal`
Clones a custom emoji from another server into your guild.

- **Usage:** `,emoji steal <emoji or URL> [name]`
- **Permissions:** `Manage Expressions`
- **Example:** `,emoji steal :hype: custom_hype`

### `emoji remove`
Deletes a custom emoji from your guild.

- **Usage:** `,emoji remove <emoji>`
- **Permissions:** `Manage Expressions`

### `emoji`
Enlarges an emoji and displays its CDN URL and internal snowflake ID.

- **Usage:** `,emoji <emoji>`

## Embed Tools

### `createembed`
Parses Bleed-style embed scripting syntax and sends the rendered embed into the channel.

- **Usage:** `,createembed <script>`
- **Aliases:** `,ce`, `,script`
- **Permissions:** `Manage Messages`
- **Example:** `,createembed {embed}$v{title: Hello}$v{description: World}`

### `embedcode`
Reverse-engineers an existing Discord embed and outputs its raw `{embed}$v...` code so you can copy or adapt it.

- **Usage:** `,embedcode <message_link>`

## Community Utilities

### `urban`
Looks up slang definitions and examples on Urban Dictionary.

- **Usage:** `,urban <query>`
- **Example:** `,urban touch grass`

### `quickpoll`
Adds quick thumbs-up / thumbs-down reactions to your message for rapid community voting.

- **Usage:** `,quickpoll` (Alias: `,qp`)

### `suggest`
Submits a suggestion to the server's review queue.

- **Usage:** `,suggest <text>`

### `donate`
Displays contribution and support links to help maintain Hollow's hosting and development infrastructure.

- **Usage:** `,donate`
