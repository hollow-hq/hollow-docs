## Playback Commands

### `play`
Searches for and enqueues songs or playlists.

- **Usage:** `,play <query or URL>`
- **Example:** `,play lofi beats to study to`
- **Supported Sources:** YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify links, and direct audio streams.

<ParamField path="query" type="string" required>
  Song title, artist name, or direct URL.
</ParamField>

---

### `skip`
Skips the currently playing song and moves to the next track in the queue.

- **Usage:** `,skip`

---

### `stop`
Stops playback, clears the queue, and disconnects Hollow from the voice channel.

- **Usage:** `,stop`

---

### `volume`
Adjusts the music output volume.

- **Usage:** `,volume <1-100>`
- **Example:** `,volume 75`

<ParamField path="volume" type="integer" required>
  Target volume percentage between 1 and 100.
</ParamField>

---

## Audio Presets (`preset`)

Apply or remove real-time DSP audio effects:

- `,preset` — Displays the menu of all available filter presets.
- `,preset 8d` — Enables 360-degree rotational stereo audio.
- `,preset nightcore` — Speeds up audio and raises pitch (1.3x).
- `,preset daycore` — Slows down audio and lowers pitch (0.7x).
- `,preset earrape` — Applies severe distortion and gain overdrive.
- `,preset off` — Clears all audio filters.
